## 5. Statistical-Rigor Improvements Now Wired In

The aligned update also changed the reporting method itself:

1. raw per-trial latency arrays are now persisted for both Enron and healthcare reruns
2. bootstrap confidence intervals are now included for workload-family summaries
3. hierarchical bootstrap confidence intervals are now included where raw trials are available
4. IQR reporting is now included to make skew visible
5. significance testing still remains workload-level by design to avoid pseudo-replication

## 6. Important Interpretation Changes

These are the main scientific changes from the aligned reruns:

1. Enron and healthcare are now on the same `100`-trial footing
2. the older mismatch between `100`-trial Enron and `10`-trial healthcare has been removed
3. the healthcare track still shows strong right-skew for several heavier families, but that skew is now reported honestly with bootstrap and IQR summaries
4. Enron `and_2` and healthcare `and_2` now have nearly identical mean total latency:
   - Enron `and_2`: `4.7145 ms`
   - Healthcare `and_2`: `4.7188 ms`
